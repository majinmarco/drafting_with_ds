Draft-Day Quick Reference

12-team ESPN PPR snake · everything from Lessons 1–3, compressed for the clock.

Your picks

Slot s, round r: odd rounds (r−1)·12 + s, even rounds r·12 − s + 1. Mid-snake you wait ~11 picks between selections; on the turns (slots 1/12) you pick twice, then wait 22. Before each pick, compute your next pick number — it decides who you can afford to wait on.

Position timing (from the 2026 scarcity curves)

Rounds 1–3: RB/WR only (exception: an elite TE — McBride/Bowers — at fair price in R2–3). Steepest curves (RB gap ~166, WR ~141): this value never comes back.
QB: wait until ~R6–8. QB curve glides (gap ~81, and QB13 is free on waivers). Exception: a top-2 QB falling 15+ picks past ADP is a genuine value pick.
TE: elite early or punt late. The middle of the TE curve is a value desert — take the cliff-top or take the glide, never pay mid-round prices for it.
K and D/ST: your last two picks. No exceptions. Both are ~replacement-level everywhere and streamable weekly.
Bench (7 spots): buy upside, not safety. Late picks should be lottery tickets — ambiguous backfields, rookie WRs, your RB1's direct backup. A "safe" 140-point veteran is replacement level with extra steps. Never a second K or D/ST.

Every pick, in order (30 seconds)

  1. Filter the cheat-sheet table to available players; sort by VOR.
  2. Check the edge of your top 2–3 candidates: if a target's ADP is well after your next pick number, he'll likely still be there — take the scarcer player now instead.
  3. Check roster shape: can you still fill every starting slot with real value? (2 RB + 2 WR + flex ≈ aim for 5 combined RB/WR by round 6.)
  4. Pick. Don't reach on names — news and hype are already in ADP, and ADP is already in your plan.

Traps

Position runs: when four QBs go in a row, the room is panicking — that's when the RB/WR value falls to you. Follow the sheet, not the room. Bye weeks: ignore them on draft day except avoiding QB = TE bye if you punt both. Your own fandom: the sheet doesn't know your favorite team; that's its virtue.
