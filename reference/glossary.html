Reference

Course Glossary

Every term the lessons use, in one place. Print-friendly.

The game

Head-to-head (H2H) points
League format where each week your roster's total points are compared against one opponent's; more points = a win. Your league's format.
Starting lineup / starters
The subset of your roster that scores in a given week. ESPN default: 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, 1 FLEX, 1 D/ST, 1 K.
FLEX
A lineup slot that accepts an RB, WR, or TE — filled by your best remaining player among those positions.
Bench
Rostered players not in the starting lineup. They score nothing but provide injury/bye-week cover and upside bets.
D/ST
Defense/Special Teams — you start an entire NFL team's defense, scoring for sacks, turnovers, return TDs, points allowed.
PPR (points per reception)
Scoring where each catch is worth 1 point (ESPN default; "fractional" = partial points for yardage, e.g. 0.1/rushing yard). Inflates the value of pass-catchers.
Bye week
The one week each NFL team doesn't play; its players score zero. Why benches and positional depth matter.
Waiver wire
The pool of undrafted/dropped players you can add during the season. The practical embodiment of "replacement level."

The draft

Snake draft
Draft where pick order reverses each round (1→12, then 12→1). Compensates for early-slot advantage.
Draft slot
Your fixed position (1–12) in the round-1 order. Determines all your pick numbers: odd rounds (r−1)·12+s, even rounds r·12−s+1.
The turn
Slots 1 and 12, which pick twice back-to-back and then wait 22 picks.
ADP (average draft position)
The average overall pick number at which a player is drafted across many real drafts. The market price of a player, and your best model of opponent behavior.
Mock draft
A practice draft against humans or bots (ESPN lobby, FantasyPros simulator). The rehearsal arena for any strategy.
Cheat sheet
Your prepared, ordered draft list — in this course, generated from data: players ranked by VOR with ADP alongside.
Reach / value pick
Drafting a player well before his ADP (reach) or well after it (value). Meaningful only relative to the market.

The math

Projection
A model's estimate of a player's total (or weekly) fantasy points this season, in a specific scoring system.
Replacement level
The expected output of the best player at a position you could get without spending a meaningful pick — approximated by the "last starter" (QB12, RB24, WR24, TE12 in a 12-team default league) or best undrafted player.
VOR / VORP (value over replacement)
A player's projection minus his position's replacement level. The number that makes players comparable across positions.
VBD (value-based drafting)
The strategy of making each pick by VOR rather than raw projected points.
Positional scarcity
How steeply production falls as you go down a position's rankings. Steep (RB, WR) = draft early; flat (QB, TE, K, D/ST) = wait.
Scarcity curve
Plot of projected points vs positional rank. Its slope is scarcity; you drew the 2026 curves in Lesson 2.
Baseline
The rank chosen to define replacement level (e.g. RB24). The key modeling decision in any VOR system.
