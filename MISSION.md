# Mission: Data-Driven Snake Drafting for Fantasy Football

## Why
Marco wants to win his 12-team head-to-head ESPN snake-draft league this season **and** use it as a real-world proving ground for data science and optimization skills (simulation, value modeling, linear programming). A draft strategy he built himself, with pandas, should beat gut-feel drafters at the table.

## Success looks like
- Walk into the 2026 draft with a self-built, VOR-based cheat sheet generated from real projection data in pandas
- Explain *why* each pick is right (replacement value, positional scarcity, ADP market gaps) — not just follow rankings
- Run a Monte Carlo draft simulation / optimizer and use its output live during the draft
- Finish the season having internalized transferable DS skills: baseline modeling, simulation, sequential decision optimization

## Constraints
- Draft is imminent — NFL 2026 season starts ~Sep 10, so early lessons must be draft-usable fast; deeper optimization theory can continue after draft day
- New to fantasy football itself: game concepts must be taught, but briskly (strong general quant intuition)
- Comfortable with Python/pandas; NOT yet experienced with optimization libraries
- League: 12-team, H2H points, snake draft, ESPN (default PPR fractional scoring assumed — verify league settings)

## Out of scope
- Dynasty/keeper leagues, auction drafts, DFS (DraftKings/FanDuel)
- In-season management (waivers, trades, start/sit) — until the draft is done
- Betting markets
